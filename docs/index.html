Draw In The Air & Let AI Guess

Pinch your thumb and index finger together to draw with your webcam. Pick a color, sketch a digit, a letter, or a doodle, then hit Guess — a neural network trained on real handwriting and sketch data will classify your final drawing. Runs entirely in your browser, no server, video never leaves your device.

Needs camera access. The AI model (~2MB) loads once, then everything runs locally.
